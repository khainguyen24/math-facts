# Math Facts Trainer

Flash card drills for the multiplication tables, division facts, adding and subtracting whole numbers up to 1,000, adding and subtracting decimals, and adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing fractions and mixed numbers. The whole app is one page, `index.html`, and it runs in any browser with no install.

**Open it:** https://khainguyen24.github.io/math-facts/

On an iPad, open the link in Safari, tap Share › Add to Home Screen, and it opens like an app. Progress is saved on that device.

## Features
- Times tables & division: multiplication, division or mixed; any of the 1–12 tables, up to ×10 or ×12
- Adding & subtracting to 1,000: adding, subtracting or mixed; numbers up to 20, 100 or 1,000; carrying and borrowing any, always or never; stacked by place value or in a line
- Adding & subtracting decimals: 1 to 4 decimal places, same or different places in each number, whole part under 10, 100, 1,000 or 10,000, shown stacked with the decimal points lined up or in a line; a "." key on the keypad
- Fractions & mixed numbers: add, subtract, multiply, divide or a mix; plain fractions, mixed numbers or both; same or different denominators for + and −; denominators up to 6, 10 or 12. Answers are typed with "and" and "over" keys (1 and 3 over 4 is 1 3/4) and shown as a real fraction. In simplest-form mode an equal but unsimplified answer gets a nudge, not a miss
- Type the answer on the on-screen keypad, or flip the card and grade yourself
- Missed cards come back a few cards later; "Drill missed facts" after each round
- Players, each with their own settings, 12×12 mastery grid, round history and progress charts
- "Mix in facts I missed before" replays earlier mistakes inside a new drill
- Write right on the card (carries, borrowing, the answer under the line) with a finger, Apple Pencil or mouse. Writing grows the card down over the number pad for room to work; a dim chevron at the bottom of the card brings the pad back. It clears for each new card
- Scratchpad drawer for longer working-out
- Sound effects built into the page, so it also works offline

## Updating
Edit `index.html` and push to `main`; GitHub Pages republishes it in a minute or two.
